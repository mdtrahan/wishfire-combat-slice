## Packing

Scratch `art/_work/ground-pound/pack.py` cuts painted source cels, aligns their ground contacts, resamples with Lanczos and packs horizontal strips. It generates no procedural effect strokes or primitive artwork. Eruption masters each have eight source poses; output uses source indices [0,1,3,4,5,6,7] for seven readable frames. Variant B received a generated edit to replace standing late slabs with airborne breakup and dust. The fist sheet received a generated layout edit; packing uses measured per-cel crop boundaries. The returned source grids were not perfectly aligned, so explicit crop boxes are retained in scratch.

Source alpha is remapped by clamp((alpha-8)/247,0,1). Tail opacity envelopes: fist [1,1,1,.78,.4,.1]; both eruption variants [1,1,1,.92,.7,.38,.1]; finale [1,1,1,1,.86,.60,.3,.08]. RGB is zero wherever exported alpha is zero. No premultiplication or palette quantization. Three clear texels inside every cel edge, exact frame-width stride. All supplied textures are RGBA PNG; JPEGs are preview composites only.
