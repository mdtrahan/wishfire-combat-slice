# Mirewing self-review

Candidate complete for lead art-director review. No runtime integration or independent approval claimed.

- **Concept match:** Visible face, tubular jade mouth, lime glands with plum spots, diamond wings, dangling legs, navy wrap, oxblood fastening and gold trim come from the approved full-resolution paint. `vs-concept.png` compares idle 000 directly with the approved runtime image on both grounds. The puppet is independently resampled from full resolution; its raster placement and shadow width differ slightly from the supplied 128px image. Generated paint is restricted to hidden attachment tissue.
- **Flight:** Both wing bones flap in every airborne clip while the body bobs ±1.4 runtime pixels. Idle uses two flap cycles per second-long clip; walk uses three, a five-degree forward lean and small forward drift. Attack keeps the flap while the glands swell 12% and the rooted mouth stretches forward. Cast swells the glands 23%. Hit recoils backward and recovers. Defeat deliberately damps flap and bob into folded, grounded rest. Spawn descends four runtime pixels into its bobbing hover. Contact sheets show every frame in order.
- **Grounded shadow:** Separate root-parented shadow remains centred at (64,125), independent of the body. All airborne clips have pixel-identical ground-region hashes across frames. Defeat brings the feet/body down into the shadow; the shadow itself stays fixed. No scaling was needed.
- **Wing and joint seams:** Initial decomposition exposed contour strips and a mouth gap. Those were corrected by assigning isolated contour fragments to the body, cleaning alpha below 12/255, and stretching the mouth around its fixed base. Final 3x contact sheets show no open joint cracks at runtime size. Native 1024px proofs expose the cutout construction around the shoulder and far-wing root; the overlap edge still changes with the flap. Near upper/lower wings share one bone, so the fold reads as a paired fin rather than two independently bending membranes. Tiny legs remain attached to body paint.
- **Canvas:** All 144 straight-alpha 128×128 frames pass alpha≥16 bounds checks with a visible margin. Spawn entry was shortened after the first bounds check caught a clipped wing tip. Both facings have the required counts: idle 12, walk 12, attack 9, cast 12, hit 6, defeated 12, spawn 9. Every right frame is an exact horizontal mirror. Native rigs retain editable parents and bone links.
- **58px readability:** `readability-<state>-58px.png` provides every frame at exactly 58px on sand and #101116. Teal wing shape, bright poison glands and left-facing mouth remain distinct. Gold trim, fastening and tiny claws merge into small accents at this size. Cast's gland swell remains visible; basic-shot gland preparation is subtler and the mouth extension carries the action.
- **Timing and limits:** All frame holds are 1000/12 ms; only idle/walk loop. The actor manifest follows the current packer's semantic `basic/hurt/ko` keys while retaining requested `attack/hit/defeated` frame filenames. A fixed animation clip cannot enforce the 3s firing interval or poison effect; those remain simulation-owned. The projectile is absent as requested. Short spawn travel and compressed defeat wings are deliberate accommodations to the fixed canvas. The editable right SIF has Synfig’s one-author-pixel mirror sampling offset at 1024px (0.125 runtime pixels); exported right PNGs use exact pixel mirrors. Native GUI editing and live battle playback were not exercised.
